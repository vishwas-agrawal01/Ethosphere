About Ethosphere

A new studio, built around one journey done right.

Ethosphere is a young studio for spiritual travel across India — founded on the belief that one journey, done with total care, matters more than a long catalog done at a distance.

A studio for spiritual travel, not a travel agency.

We started Ethosphere to design journeys the way we’d want to experience them ourselves — built around presence, not itineraries. Shyamarpan, our founding retreat across Khatu Shyam Ji, Salasar Balaji and Harsh Parvat, is where that belief is being tested first: darshan arranged with care, evenings held rather than rushed, and every detail managed so a guest carries nothing but their intention.

01 Our Beginning

Started with Shyamarpan.

Our first retreat was built around three places that have shaped devotion for generations. Getting that one journey right — every transfer, every darshan, every evening — is what everything else at Ethosphere will be measured against.

02 What’s Next

A small team, deliberately.

Ethosphere stays intentionally small by design. As Shyamarpan grows, future journeys will be added the same way this one was built — slowly, and only when we can do them properly.

What we believe, in practice.

Presence over pace

Practitioner-led

Small by design
