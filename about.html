About Ethosphere

Built by people who kept going back.

Ethosphere began as a handful of return trips to the same handful of temples — long before it was a company.

A studio for spiritual travel, not a travel agency.

We started Ethosphere after a decade of independently organising small pilgrimages for friends, colleagues and eventually strangers who asked to come along. What set those trips apart was never the itinerary — it was the relationships: the priest who’d let us sit through a private aarti, the historian who’d walk us through Hampi at dawn before the heat and the crowds. Ethosphere formalises that access without losing what made it worth having in the first place.
