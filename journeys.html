Day 01 — Arrival & Darshan

  • Pick-up and assembly at Jaipur Airport
  • Journey to Nowal Nature Care by luxury coach
  • Lunch at Aapno Rajasthan
  • Traditional welcome and check-in
  • Darshan at Khatu Shyam Ji
  • Networking theme dinner

Day 02 — Yoga, Salasar & Sundowner

  • Yoga and meditation
  • Breakfast amidst nature
  • Journey to Salasar Balaji
  • Darshan at Salasar Balaji Temple
  • Prasad lunch hosted by the temple trust
  • Journey to Harsh Parvat
  • Spiritual sundowner with high tea and music
  • Return to Nowal Nature Care
  • Bhajan clubbing and dinner

Day 03 — Wellness & Farewell

  • Deep meditation at the pool
  • One-on-one health consultation
  • Spa and signature therapies
  • Check-out and farewell
  • Journey to Jaipur Airport for departure
