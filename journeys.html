Shyamarpan

A journey of surrender

Duration11 Days
RouteVaranasi · Vrindavan · Ganges
Best Suited ForThe Seeker

Eleven days moving through the ghats of Varanasi and the groves of Vrindavan — an itinerary built around surrender, not sightseeing. Shyamarpan is not a tour of temples. It is an invitation to be met by something older than yourself, at the pace silence requires.

Days 1–3 Arrival in Varanasi. Settling into the rhythm of the ghats — dawn boat rituals on the Ganges, evening Ganga aarti, and long unhurried mornings with a resident ritual guide.
Days 4–7 Travel to Vrindavan. Time inside temple courtyards and groves, private audience with local priest families, and stillness built deliberately into every day.
Days 8–11 Return to Varanasi for closing rites at the river. A final, silent morning at the ghat where the journey began — held, not rushed.
