Shyamarpan.

Across sacred cities and timeless traditions, Shyamarpan is an invitation to surrender, to connect and to experience the divine in its most intimate form.

The Invitation

Shyamarpan

Reverence. Refinement. Rhythm.

Duration3 Days · 2 Nights
RouteKhatu Shyam Ji · Salasar Balaji · Harsh Parvat
Next Departure2–4 Oct 2026

Some journeys are planned. This one is answered. A journey where the soul slows down and Divinity draws closer — where desert light, soulful music and quiet indulgence become part of the offering. An exquisite retreat, curated for darshan, devotion and the art of surrender.

Day 01 — Arrival & Darshan

  • Pick-up and assembly at Jaipur Airport
  • Journey to Nowal Nature Care by luxury coach
  • Lunch at Aapno Rajasthan
  • Traditional welcome and check-in
  • Darshan at Khatu Shyam Ji
  • Networking theme dinner

Day 02 — Yoga, Salasar & Sundowner

  • Yoga and meditation
  • Breakfast amidst nature
  • Journey to Salasar Balaji
  • Darshan at Salasar Balaji Temple
  • Prasad lunch hosted by the temple trust
  • Journey to Harsh Parvat
  • Spiritual sundowner with high tea and music
  • Return to Nowal Nature Care
  • Bhajan clubbing and dinner

Day 03 — Wellness & Farewell

  • Deep meditation at the pool
  • One-on-one health consultation
  • Spa and signature therapies
  • Check-out and farewell
  • Journey to Jaipur Airport for departure

The Journey Includes

  • Luxury coach transfers throughout
  • Two nights at Nowal Nature Care
  • All vegetarian meals
  • Assisted darshan at both shrines
  • Sundowner at Harsh Parvat
  • Bhajan evening with light & sound
  • Yoga, meditation and spa
  • Retreat kit and doctor on call

Not Included

  • Flights to and from Jaipur
  • Personal expenses and shopping
  • Therapies beyond those listed
  • Beverages outside the set menu
  • Anything not listed above

Curated stays · All-inclusive experience. Tailored arrangements available for larger groups, with stay customisation on request.
