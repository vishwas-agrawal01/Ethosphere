Privacy Policy

Draft policy — pending legal review before publication.

Last updated: 19 August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry through our website, we collect the information you provide directly: your full name, email address, phone number, journey of interest, approximate travel dates, group size, and any message or details you choose to share with us.

We do not use tracking cookies or analytics scripts on this site beyond what is strictly necessary to serve pages and process form submissions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit solely to respond to your enquiry, discuss and plan your journey, and follow up regarding your interest in travelling with Ethosphere. We may use your email or phone number to contact you about your specific enquiry.

We do not use your information for automated marketing without your consent, and we do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

3. How Your Information Is Stored

Enquiry form submissions are processed through Formspree, a third-party form-handling service, and delivered to our team’s email inbox. We take reasonable steps to keep this information secure, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is entirely infallible.

We retain enquiry information only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you and, where a journey is booked, to administer that booking.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Formspree to process enquiry form submissions and Cloudflare to host this website. These providers may process your data on our behalf as part of delivering their service, under their own respective privacy and security practices.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you have shared with us at any time by contacting us using the details below. We will respond to reasonable requests within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, please write to us at hello@ethosphere.co.in.
