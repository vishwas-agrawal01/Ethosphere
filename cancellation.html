Cancellation & Refund Policy

Draft policy — pending legal review before publication.

Last updated: 19 August 2026

1. Refund Schedule

Because each journey involves advance commitments to accommodations, temple trusts and local partners, cancellations are refunded on a tiered schedule based on how far in advance you cancel before departure:

Time Before DepartureRefund
30+ days before departureFull refund, less any non-recoverable third-party costs already incurred
15–29 days before departure50% refund
7–14 days before departure25% refund
Within 7 days of departureNo refund

2. How to Request a Cancellation

To cancel a confirmed booking, email us at paypal.vishwas@gmail.com with your name and journey details. Your cancellation is effective from the date we confirm receipt of your request in writing, not the date you send it.

3. Exceptional Circumstances

We understand that genuine emergencies happen. If you need to cancel due to a serious medical or family emergency, let us know — we’re always happy to discuss your individual circumstances and will consider reasonable flexibility on a case-by-case basis, at our discretion.

4. Changes By Ethosphere

In the rare event that Ethosphere must cancel a journey (for example, due to insufficient group size, safety conditions, or circumstances beyond our control), guests will be offered a full refund or the option to transfer to an alternative departure date.

5. Non-Refundable Items

Any flights, visas, insurance or personal expenses arranged independently by the guest are not covered by this policy and are subject to the cancellation terms of the relevant provider.
